# Privacy Policy — Screen Distance

_Last updated: July 1, 2026_

Screen Distance is designed to be completely private. Everything happens on your
device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on any server.

## 1. Camera

Screen Distance uses the front TrueDepth camera **only** to measure how far your
face is from the screen, in real time. **No photo or video is ever recorded,
saved, or transmitted.** The camera feed is used solely to read the
face-to-screen distance and is never written to disk or sent anywhere.

## 2. Data We Collect

**None leaves your device.** Screen Distance does not collect any personal data.
There is no account, no sign-up, and no analytics.

## 3. Eye-Care Statistics

Your daily distance and alert statistics are stored only in the app's private
storage on your device. They are never uploaded and are used only to draw your
in-app charts. You can remove them at any time by deleting the app.

## 4. Network

Screen Distance works completely offline and makes no network requests of any
kind.

## 5. Third Parties

Screen Distance contains no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

## 6. Children

Screen Distance is safe for children. It collects no data from anyone, including
children, and is intended to help protect young eyes from screen strain.
