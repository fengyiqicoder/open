# Support — Screen Distance

## What it does

Screen Distance helps you and your children keep a healthy distance from the
screen. Using the front TrueDepth camera, it measures how far your face is from
your device in real time and gently warns you whenever you lean in too close.
The camera is used only to read distance — no photo or video is ever recorded,
saved, or sent anywhere.

## Quick Start

1. **On first launch**, tap **Enable camera & start** and allow camera access.
2. **Hold your device normally** — the ring shows your current distance in
   centimeters. It stays green at a healthy distance and turns red when you are
   too close.
3. **Lean in too close** and you'll get a gentle vibration to back off.
4. Tap **Stop** any time to pause monitoring.

## Settings

- **Alert distance**: set your threshold anywhere from 20 to 50 cm.
- **Vibration / sound alerts**: choose how you're nudged.
- **20-20-20 reminders**: every 20 minutes, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds.

## Eye Care charts

Tap the **chart icon** (top-right) to see your average distance and alerts over
the last seven days.

## Frequently Asked

**Is my camera recorded?** No. The camera is used only to compute the
face-to-screen distance. No image, photo, or video is ever stored or sent.

**Which devices are supported?** Any iPhone or iPad with a TrueDepth front
camera (iPhone X or later), running iOS 17 or later.

**Does it work offline?** Completely. Screen Distance never uses the network.
