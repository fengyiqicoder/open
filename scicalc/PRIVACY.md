# Privacy Policy — Sci Calc (Scientific Calculator)

_Last updated: 2026-05-25_

Sci Calc ("we", "our", "us") is a calculator that respects your privacy. This
Privacy Policy explains what data Sci Calc handles, how it is used, and what
choices you have.

**Summary in one sentence**: Sci Calc collects nothing, transmits nothing,
and stores everything locally on your device.

## 1. Data we collect

**None.** Sci Calc does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal
data with us, our partners, or third parties.

Specifically, the app does **not** access or collect:

- Your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials
- Location data (GPS, IP-based, or otherwise)
- Camera, microphone, or contacts
- Advertising identifiers
- Health, fitness, motion, or sensor data
- Usage analytics or crash reports

## 2. Data stored on your device

These items are stored **locally on your device** and never leave it:

| Item | Why it exists |
|---|---|
| Calculation history (the last 50 expressions and their results) | So you can re-load a previous calculation |
| Angle mode preference (DEG / RAD) | So your last choice persists across launches |
| INV mode toggle state | Same |

All stored in iOS's local UserDefaults. No background services touch it.

## 4. Network activity

Sci Calc makes **no network requests of any kind**. The app:

- Does not contact our servers (there are no servers)
- Does not load any web content
- Does not include any third-party analytics SDKs
- Does not include any advertising SDKs

You can verify this by enabling iOS's network privacy report and looking
under Sci Calc — the list will be empty.

## 5. Data sharing

We do **not** sell, rent, share, or transfer any data to any third party.
There is nothing to share, because nothing is collected.

## 6. Your rights and choices

- **Clear history**: delete the app to remove all local data (iOS will
  remove the app's sandbox completely).

## 7. Children

Sci Calc is rated 4+ and does not collect any data, so it is safe for users
of any age.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If we ever add functionality that requires collecting data, this policy will
be updated and the in-app "What's New" notes will explain the change before
it takes effect. As of this version: nothing changes the no-collection
guarantee above.
