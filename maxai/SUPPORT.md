# Support — AI Chat

Need help with AI Chat? You're in the right place.

## Quick start

1. Open **AI Chat**.
2. Type a message in the box at the bottom and press **⌘↩** (or click the send button). Press **↩** alone for a new line.
3. Watch the reply stream in. Use the **＋ / New Chat** button (⌘N) to start a fresh conversation — your chats are saved in the sidebar.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need an account or API key?**
No. Just open the app and start typing — everything works out of the box.

**Q: What are the roles (Assistant, Translate, Rewrite, Summarize, Code)?**
Pick a role at the top of a conversation to focus the assistant on a task — for example, **Translate** turns your text into another language, and **Code** helps with programming and formats code blocks. You can change the role per conversation.

**Q: What is the "Thoughts" / "Thinking…" section?**
The model reasons before answering. You can expand the collapsible **Thoughts** section to see that reasoning, or just read the final answer.

**Q: How do I copy a reply or a code block?**
Each reply has a **Copy** button, and every code block has its own copy button in its top-right corner.

**Q: Is my conversation private?**
Your chats are stored only on your Mac. To generate replies, the messages you send are transmitted to our AI provider (OpenRouter). Please avoid entering sensitive personal information. See our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).

**Q: A reply looks wrong or inappropriate.**
AI replies can be inaccurate. Use the **Report** button under any reply to flag it to us. The app is rated 17+.

**Q: Which languages are supported?**
The assistant replies in the language you write in. The app's interface is available in Dutch, Danish, Swedish, Finnish, Czech, Polish, Greek and English.
