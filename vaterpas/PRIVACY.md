# Privacy Policy — Vaterpas — Level & Protractor

_Last updated: 2026-06-01_

Vaterpas — Level & Protractor ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. Vaterpas — Level & Protractor is a bubble level and angle protractor using the device motion sensors. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## 1. Data we collect

**None.** Vaterpas — Level & Protractor does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics.

## 2. Where data is stored

All app data and settings stay **locally on your iPhone**. We do not run a server, use iCloud sync, or use any third-party storage.

## 3. Permissions

| Permission | Used? | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Motion sensors | ✅ | Read tilt to show the level and angle (no permission prompt; not Motion & Fitness) |
| Location | ❌ | Not used |
| Camera | ❌ | Not used |
| Microphone | ❌ | Not used |
| Contacts | ❌ | Not used |
| Photos | ❌ | Not used |
| Network / Internet | ❌ | Runs fully offline |

## 4. Data sharing

We do **not** sell, rent, share, or transfer any data to any third party. No analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, no data brokers — ever.

## 5. Children

This app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

## 6. Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves; the date above reflects the latest version.
