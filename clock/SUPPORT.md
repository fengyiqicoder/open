# Support — Часовник — Clock

Часовник — Clock is a minimalist full-screen display clock with gradient themes and day/year progress bars.

## Quick Start

1. **Open the app** — everything works on your iPhone, no account or sign-in needed.
2. **Use the main features** straight away; settings are available in-app.
3. **Offline** — the app needs no internet connection for its core features.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. Часовник — Clock requires no registration or login. Everything runs locally.

**Does it collect my data?**
No. No data is collected, uploaded, or shared. There are no ads and no tracking.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes — all core features work without an internet connection.
