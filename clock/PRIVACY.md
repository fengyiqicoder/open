# Privacy Policy — Часовник — Clock

_Last updated: 2026-06-01_

Часовник — Clock ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. Часовник — Clock is a minimalist full-screen display clock with gradient themes and day/year progress bars. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## 1. Data we collect

**None.** Часовник — Clock does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics.

## 2. Where data is stored

All app data and settings stay **locally on your iPhone**. We do not run a server, use iCloud sync, or use any third-party storage.

## 4. Data sharing

We do **not** sell, rent, share, or transfer any data to any third party. No analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, no data brokers — ever.

## 5. Children

This app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
