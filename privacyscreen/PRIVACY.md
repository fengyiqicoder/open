# Privacy Policy — Privacy Screen

_Last updated: 2026-05-24_

Privacy Screen ("we", "our", "us") is built on a single principle: an app
designed to protect your privacy should not be a source of privacy risk
itself. This Privacy Policy explains what data Privacy Screen handles, how
it is used, and what choices you have.

**Summary in one sentence**: Privacy Screen collects nothing, transmits
nothing, and stores everything locally on your device.

## 1. Data we collect

**None.** Privacy Screen does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any
personal data with us, our partners, or third parties.

Specifically, the app does **not** access or collect:

- Your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials
- Location data (GPS, IP-based, or otherwise)
- Camera, microphone, or contacts
- Advertising identifiers
- Health, fitness, motion, or sensor data
- Usage analytics or crash reports

## 2. Data stored on your device

These items are stored **locally on your device** and never leave it:

| Item | Why it exists |
|---|---|
| Your selected cover image | So the cover appears instantly when you trigger Privacy Screen |
| The reference to a built-in preset, or a copy of a photo you picked | To remember your choice across app launches |
| App preferences (trigger setup state, onboarding completion) | So the app remembers you've configured it |

If you pick a photo from your Photos library, a copy is stored inside the
app's sandbox container — iOS itself isolates this from other apps. We
never see the image; it never leaves your device.

## 4. Network activity

Privacy Screen makes **no network requests of any kind**. The app:

- Does not contact our servers (there are no servers)
- Does not load any web content
- Does not include any third-party analytics SDKs
- Does not include any advertising SDKs

You can verify this by enabling iOS's network privacy report and looking
under Privacy Screen — the list will be empty.

## 5. Data sharing

We do **not** sell, rent, share, or transfer any data to any third party.
There is nothing to share, because nothing is collected.

## 6. Your rights and choices

- **Change your cover image**: open the app, pick a new one.
- **Remove a photo you imported**: pick a different preset, or delete the
  app — iOS removes all sandboxed data automatically.
- **Stop using Privacy Screen**: delete the app. All local data goes with it.

## 7. Children

Privacy Screen is rated 4+ and does not collect any data, so it is safe for
users of any age. However, the app is most useful for users who own their
own iPhone and can configure system triggers.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If we ever add functionality that requires collecting data, this policy will
be updated and the in-app "What's New" notes will explain the change before
it takes effect. As of this version: nothing changes the no-collection
guarantee above.
