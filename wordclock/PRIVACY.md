# Privacy Policy — Word Clock

_Last updated: July 1, 2026_

Word Clock is designed to be completely private. Everything happens on your
device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on any server.

## 1. Data We Collect

**None.** Word Clock does not collect any personal data. There is no account,
no sign-up, and no analytics.

## 2. Your Settings

Your theme and display preferences are stored only in the app's private storage
on your device. They never leave your device.

## 3. Network

Word Clock works completely offline and makes no network requests of any kind.

## 4. Third Parties

Word Clock contains no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

## 5. Children

Word Clock is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone.
