# Support — Word Clock

## What it does

Word Clock tells the time in glowing words instead of digits. A grid of letters
lights up to spell the current time — IT IS HALF PAST TEN — as a calm,
typographic clock for your desk, shelf, or nightstand. Fully offline, no
account, no ads, no tracking.

## Quick Start

1. **Open the app** — the letter grid lights up the words for the current time.
2. **Tap the clock face** to hide every control for a clean, full-screen clock.
   Tap again to bring the controls back.
3. **Tap the moon icon** (top-left) for nightstand mode: the screen dims and
   stays awake, so it stays readable all night.
4. **Tap the sliders icon** (top-right) to open themes and display options.

## Themes & Display

- Choose from six themes: Midnight, Ember, Mint, Paper, Rose, and Mono.
- Toggle the digital time, date, blinking seconds dot, and letter glow.
- Switch the digital readout between 12-hour and 24-hour.

## Frequently Asked

**Does it keep the screen on?** Yes. While the app is open the screen stays
awake so it works as a bedside or desk clock. Use nightstand mode to dim it.

**Does it work offline?** Completely. Word Clock never uses the network.

**Why does the time round to five-minute words?** That's how a classic word
clock reads — it lights the nearest phrase such as "ten past" or "quarter to".
