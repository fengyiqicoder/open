# Privacy Policy — Signature Creator

_Last updated: June 13, 2026_

Signature Creator is designed to be completely private. Everything happens on
your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on any
server.

## 1. Data We Collect

**None.** Signature Creator does not collect any personal data. There is no
account, no sign-up, and no analytics.

## 2. Signatures and Documents

Signatures you create and PDFs you sign are stored only in the app's private
storage on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere and are never shared
unless you explicitly export and share them yourself.

## 3. Network

Signature Creator makes **no network requests of any kind**. The app works
fully offline.

## 4. Permissions

| Permission | Used? |
|---|---|
| Camera | ❌ No |
| Photos | ❌ No |
| Microphone | ❌ No |
| Location | ❌ No |
| Contacts | ❌ No |
| Notifications | ❌ No |

## 5. Third-Party Services

The app contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising, and no tracking of any
kind.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## 7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data, because we do not collect any.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a
new "Last updated" date.
