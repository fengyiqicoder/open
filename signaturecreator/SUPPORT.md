# Support — Signature Creator

## What it does

Signature Creator lets you make a signature on your iPhone and drop it onto a
PDF — entirely offline. Draw your signature with a finger or type your name in
a handwriting style, save it, then open any PDF and place the signature exactly
where you need it. Export the signed PDF and share it. No account, no cloud, no
ads, no tracking.

## Quick Start

1. **Create a signature**: tap **Create Signature** → choose **Draw** (sign with
   your finger) or **Type** (type your name and pick a handwriting style) →
   **Save**. It's stored in your on-device library.
2. **Sign a document**: tap **Sign a Document** → pick a PDF from Files.
3. **Place your signature**: tap **Add Signature**, choose one from your library,
   then drag, pinch to resize, and rotate it into position. Tap **Place**.
4. **Export**: tap **Export** to save or share the signed PDF.

## Creating a signature

- **Draw**: use the canvas to sign with your finger. Pick black, blue, or red
  ink. Use **Undo** or **Clear** to redo.
- **Type**: type your name and swipe through the built-in handwriting fonts.
- Every signature is saved as a transparent PNG so it sits cleanly on any page.

## Signing a PDF

- Import a PDF via **Sign a Document** (Files app).
- Add a saved signature, then move / resize / rotate it on the page.
- Multi-page PDFs: use the page arrows and place a signature on any page.
- **Export** writes the signature into the PDF and opens the share sheet.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need an account or internet connection?**
No. Signature Creator works fully offline. There is no sign-up and no login.

**Does the app use my camera?**
No. You create a signature by drawing or typing.

**Where are my signatures and documents stored?**
Only on your device, in the app's private storage. Nothing is uploaded.

**Does the app send any data to a server?**
No. The app makes no network requests of any kind.

**Does it show ads or track me?**
No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

**Is the signed PDF a real PDF?**
Yes. The signature is written into the PDF and can be opened in any PDF reader.
