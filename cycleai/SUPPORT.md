# Support — Cycle AI Period & PMS Tracker

## Quick Start

1. **Log your first period**: tap **+ Log today** on the home screen, then
   select the day and flow intensity (None / Light / Medium / Heavy).
2. **Track symptoms**: in the Log screen, tap any symptom chips (cramps,
   bloating, headache, mood emoji, etc.).
3. **Add basal body temperature**: optional — enter °C or °F in the BBT field.
4. **See predictions**: after just one logged cycle, Cycle AI will start
   forecasting. Predictions sharpen with each cycle you log.

## Why are early predictions wide?

Until you've logged 2-3 cycles, Cycle AI uses a 25-day population average
with ±5 days confidence. Tap **"Tap to see why"** on the home card to view
the probability curve and exactly how the prediction was built.

## How accurate is the AI?

Accuracy improves with data:
- 0 logged cycles → ±5 days, population baseline
- 2 logged cycles → ±3 days, your average length
- 6+ logged cycles → ±1-2 days, personalized model

The app always shows the confidence band — you'll never be surprised.

## Phase guidance — what is it?

Your cycle has four phases (menstrual, follicular, ovulation, luteal), each
with different hormone levels. Tap **Today** → phase card to see
recommendations for eating, moving, and self-care during the current phase.
These are general wellness suggestions, not medical advice.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does my data leave my phone?**
No. Cycle data stays on-device. See our [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY.md).

**Can I sync between devices?**
Yes — enable iCloud Sync in iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Cycle AI.
Sync goes through Apple's encrypted iCloud, not our servers.

**How do I delete all data?**
Settings → Delete All Data. Permanent and instant.

**Is Cycle AI a medical device?**
No. Cycle AI is a wellness tracker and should not be used as medical advice
or as a contraceptive method. Consult a healthcare provider for medical
questions.

**Is it free?**
Yes, with optional premium features. We never show ads.
