# Privacy Policy — Cycle AI Period & PMS Tracker

_Last updated: 2026-05-24_

Cycle AI ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains
what data Cycle AI collects, how it is used, and what choices you have. By using
Cycle AI you agree to the practices described below.

## 1. Data we collect

Cycle AI collects only what is needed to provide cycle predictions and logging.

| Category | Examples | Why we need it |
|---|---|---|
| Cycle data | period start/end dates, flow intensity | Generate next-period & ovulation forecasts |
| Symptom data | cramps, bloating, headache, mood, etc. | Phase guidance + symptom trends |
| Body measurements | basal body temperature | Fertility-awareness predictions |
| App preferences | dark mode, °C/°F, locale | App settings |

We do **not** collect:
- Your name, email, phone number, or account credentials
- Photos, contacts, location, microphone, or camera data
- Advertising identifiers

## 2. Where data is stored

Your cycle data is stored **locally on your device**. We do not run a server
that mirrors your data. If you enable iCloud Sync (optional, via iOS Settings),
Apple's iCloud encrypts and stores a backup; we never see that backup.

## 3. How we use data

Data is used **only on-device** to:
- Predict your next period and ovulation window
- Display phase-specific recommendations
- Show your historical calendar
- Improve forecast accuracy as you log more cycles

We do **not** send your data to any server for training, analytics, or
advertising. All AI computations run on your device.

## 4. Data sharing

We do **not** sell, rent, share, or transfer your personal data to any third
party. No analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, no data brokers — ever.

## 5. Your rights

You can at any time:
- **Export** your data via Settings → Export Data
- **Delete** all data via Settings → Delete All Data (instant + permanent)
- **Disable** iCloud Sync via iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Cycle AI

If you delete the app, all local data is removed by iOS.

## 6. Children

Cycle AI is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect
data from children under 13.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be
announced in the app's "What's New" notes. The effective date at the top will
always reflect the latest version.
