# Support — Lock Keyboard

Lock Keyboard locks your keyboard with one tap so you can wipe it clean — every key does nothing until you unlock. No permissions, fully sandboxed.

## Quick Start

1. **Open the app** — no account, no sign-in, no permissions needed.
2. **Click Lock Keyboard** (or press ⌃⌥L from anywhere) to lock. A full-screen overlay confirms the keyboard is locked.
3. **Press ⌃⌥L again** to unlock. Use the cleaning timer to auto-unlock after 30s / 1m / 2m, or choose Hold to stay locked until you unlock.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need to grant any permissions?**
No. Lock Keyboard runs fully sandboxed and never asks for Accessibility or Input Monitoring.

**How do I unlock once the keyboard is locked?**
Press ⌃⌥L — the unlock hint is shown on the lock screen. (System shortcuts such as ⌘Tab are intentionally not blocked, so you can always switch apps.)

**What is the cleaning timer for?**
It auto-unlocks the keyboard after 30 seconds, 1 minute, or 2 minutes — handy for a quick wipe. Choose Hold to stay locked until you press ⌃⌥L.

**Does it collect my data?**
No. No data is collected, uploaded, or shared. There are no ads and no tracking.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes — everything runs locally with no internet connection.
