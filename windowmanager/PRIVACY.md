# Privacy Policy — Lock Keyboard

_Last updated: 2026-06-09_

Lock Keyboard ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. Lock Keyboard is a Mac app that locks your keyboard with one tap so you can wipe it clean — without typing gibberish or locking your whole screen. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## 1. Data we collect

**None.** Lock Keyboard does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics.

## 2. Where data is stored

All app data and settings stay **locally on your Mac**. We do not run a server, use iCloud sync, or use any third-party storage.

## 3. Permissions

| Permission | Used? | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Accessibility | ❌ | Not used — Lock Keyboard is fully sandboxed |
| Input Monitoring | ❌ | Not used |
| Network / Internet | ❌ | Runs fully offline |
| Location | ❌ | Not used |
| Camera | ❌ | Not used |
| Microphone | ❌ | Not used |
| Contacts | ❌ | Not used |

## 4. Data sharing

We do **not** sell, rent, share, or transfer any data to any third party. No analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, no data brokers — ever.

## 5. Children

This app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

## 6. Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves; the date above reflects the latest version.
